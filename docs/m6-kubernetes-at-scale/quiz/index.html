On a cluster with 10,981 pods, you find 11,983 events in etcd — more events than pods. What is the recognised mitigation, and whose lesson is it?

GKE publishes limits like 65,000 nodes and 300,000 pods. What is the right way to interpret such a number for your own cluster?

On a laptop control plane, a full `kubectl get pods -A` costs ~0.08s at 0 pods, ~0.5s at 1,000, ~2.2s at 5,000, ~4.9s at 10,000, then becomes unusable around ~20,000. What does this curve tell you about planning capacity for your own cluster?

A Service backed by 3,000 pods used to maintain a single Endpoints object listing all 3,000 pod IPs. Why did this become a write storm as the Service grew, and what fixes it?

A buggy controller starts flooding the API server with expensive LIST calls. Node heartbeats and leader-election traffic keep working normally, and you see HTTP 429s on the flooding controller's requests instead of a cluster-wide outage. What is happening?

You page through 5,000 pods with `--chunk-size=500` and get a `continue` token back with each page. What does that token actually encode, and why does it matter?

An informer's initial LIST specifies `resourceVersion=0` instead of leaving it unset. What does that actually change, and why do controllers rely on it?

You sort `apiserver_storage_objects` by count and find leases and events near the top, ahead of most workload objects. What should you take from that?
